# New-project interview (send in one message, defaults pre-filled)

1. The idea in one sentence. Who is it for?
2. What do they do today without it? What hurts?
3. In 3 months, what would make you say "it works"?
4. Must-haves for the first usable version; nice-to-haves for later.
5. Platforms (web, desktop, mobile, game client, CLI) and languages for users.
6. Budget. Default: no paid services; free tiers, open source, self-hosted; a domain name is OK.
7. Hosting and machines available (homelab? how many hours a day is it on? RAM?).
8. Deadlines or events.
9. Licence for code and for art. Default: MIT code, all rights reserved art.
10. Brand: name, logo, colours, a site or app whose look you like.
11. Review time per week, and when (evenings, weekends)? Phone or desktop?
12. Things you never want agents to do on their own.
